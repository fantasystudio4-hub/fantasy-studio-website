How this list works

Tap a section title (Booked, Sent, Drafts…) to open or close it. Tap a card to expand it — the status chip moves it draft → sent → booked → delivered, or not confirmed to park a quote whose event is on hold.

⚠️ Work on these first is worked out for you: money still owed, and records nothing has happened on lately. Each card says why it is there. They are moved into that group, not copied, so they will not also appear under their status below — the pill on the card still tells you what that status is. The ⏱ 12d mark turns amber after 10 days without an update and red after 20.

Calendar Festivals

Shown on the calendar — a marker on the date, the name in the day box below it. One per line as YYYY-MM-DD|Name.
Check the lunar ones. Hindu festivals follow a lunisolar calendar and Islamic ones the Hijri, so their dates move every year and an Indian observance can shift a day on a moon sighting. The seeded list is a starting point, not an authority — fixed-date holidays like Republic Day and Christmas are safe; Raksha Bandhan, Milad un Nabi, Diwali and the rest are worth a minute against a panchang.
