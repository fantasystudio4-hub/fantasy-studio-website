Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to www.fantasystudio.in and the Fantasy Studio mobile apps.

Fantasy Studio is a wedding photography and cinematography studio in Malakpet, Hyderabad, India. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website or our mobile apps, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details — when you send us an enquiry or build a package: your name, phone number, event type, event dates and any message you write.
  • Booking records — if you book us: your package details, event schedule, payments you have made to us (recorded by our team), and delivery progress.
  • Sign-in — the client, crew and partner areas sign in with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your mobile number. We store your verified number to show you your own booking and nothing else.
  • Push notifications — if you install our app and allow notifications, we store a device token so we can tell you about your own booking (confirmation, payments received, delivery updates). Declining notifications changes nothing else about the app.
  • Saved package — the package you build on our website is kept on your own device (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. It reaches us only if you send it.

What we never collect

  • No card or bank details — the app and website never process payments.
  • No location tracking, no contact-list access, no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, ever.

Where it is stored

Our website and apps run on Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Your information is stored in Google's data centres, protected by security rules that let you see only your own records, and our small team see the studio's. Google may store data on servers outside India; Google's own commitments are described in the Firebase privacy documentation.

Who can see it

Only the studio team, for running your booking. Notifications are delivered through Google's messaging service and, on iPhone, Apple's push service. We share information with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept as long as we operate, because clients return to us for copies of their memories years later. Enquiries that never become bookings are cleaned out periodically. Push tokens are deleted when you log out or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Ask us any time what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings, or just log out of the app.
  • Our services are for people planning weddings and events; they are not directed at children.
